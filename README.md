## Calendar view

Use the **Cards / Calendar** buttons at the top right of the page to switch views.
The calendar shows one week (Sunday to Saturday) with time running top to bottom.
Each activity sits at its meeting time and shows its name and enrollment (for example `5/12`).
Activities at the same time appear side by side. Hover over one to see its full details.
The search and filters on the left also apply to the calendar. Your choice is remembered.
